Brand

Mission

Our purpose and the promise we make to our customers.

Be Outmore.

Outmore Living creates heated outdoor furniture that extends life beyond four walls. We believe comfort shouldn't end at the door—it should follow you outside.

Year-round living. Outdoor moments are meant to be enjoyed in every season. Cold weather shouldn't mean retreating indoors.

Premium by design. Beautifully crafted heated furniture that seamlessly blends warmth, comfort, and sophisticated style.

Brand

Brand Voice

How we communicate with our audience.

Warm. Inviting, comfortable, and embracing. Our words create a feeling of welcome and connection.

Confident. Assured without arrogance. We know our products deliver on their promise of quality.

Refined. Elevated, sophisticated, and premium. Every word choice reflects our commitment to quality.

Clear. Direct, concise, and no fluff. We respect our audience's time and intelligence.

Photo Guidelines

Warmth and comfort. Our photography captures the feeling of outdoor living at its finest—inviting, aspirational, and genuine.

Warmth & Comfort

Architecture Integration

Lighting. Natural light preferred. Golden hour and blue hour create the warmest atmosphere. Avoid harsh midday shadows.

Composition. Generous negative space. Products should breathe within the frame. Rule of thirds for lifestyle shots.

Color Grading. Warm tones that complement our brand colors. Slightly lifted shadows. Natural, not overly processed.

Glassmorphism

Layered depth. Translucent, frosted glass effects create modern, sophisticated interfaces with visual hierarchy through blur and transparency.

Glass Card. Frosted glass on colored backgrounds.

Light Glass. Over photography for readable overlays.

Dark Glass. For dark overlay buttons and text.

.glass {
  background: hsl(var(--color-surface-overlay) / 0.7);
  backdrop-filter: blur(12px);
  -webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(12px);
  border: 1px solid hsl(var(--color-border) / 0.5);
}

Motion & Animation

Purposeful motion. Animation that enhances user experience and guides attention without overwhelming the interface.

Fade In. Elements gently appear with opacity transition. Subtle and elegant.

Slide Up. Content slides in from below with easing. Creates natural flow.

Scale In. Elements grow from slightly smaller with spring easing. Playful and engaging.
